FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about our resume builder platform.

Is this resume builder truly 100% free? ▼
Yes! You can create, edit, save, and download unlimited PDF resumes without paying a single dollar. There are no paywalls or trial periods.
Are these resume templates ATS friendly? ▼
Absolutely. All templates are built using standard semantic hierarchy, clean parseable text, and recruiter-standard typography to ensure high ATS compatibility.
Will I lose my data if I refresh the page? ▼
No. Your working resume is automatically saved to your browser's LocalStorage as a draft. You can also back up your data as a JSON file or create a free account to save it.
Can I customize the resume colors and fonts? ▼
Yes! You can choose from 6 curated color accents (including our signature Emerald Teal, Sapphire Blue, and Royale Violet) and switch between 3 distinct layouts at any time.
